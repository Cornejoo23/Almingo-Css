In progress

Learning through play

A Java application that turns learning into a game and raises awareness about sustainability. It is a collective bingo that is played in a room: everybody plays at the same time and the cards contain questions and actions related to the SDGs (Sustainable Development Goals) and IT industry.

The goal is that groups learn together while they play, and that they remember the topics better than with a normal lesson.

Completed

Orders and deliveries app

An internal application for Markutech to manage customer orders and deliveries. The staff can register a new order, follow its status until it reaches the customer and check the delivery history from a single screen.

It replaced several spreadsheets that had to be updated by hand, and it was delivered on the agreed date with a few weeks of support after the launch.

Completed

Internal tool renewal

A new application for Teika that replaced an old internal tool that had become difficult to maintain. We talked with the staff who use it every day and redesigned the workflow to make it shorter and simpler.

The result is a clean and stable application, and the number of support tickets dropped a lot after the launch.
